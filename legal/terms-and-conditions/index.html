1. ABOUT TASKA

Taska is a marketplace that connects people or businesses that need work performed (“Task

Posters”) with individuals who offer to perform those tasks (“Taskers”).

Taska provides technology and marketplace services that may include:

  • task posting;
  • task discovery and matching;
  • bidding and fixed-price task acceptance;
  • identity verification;
  • messaging;
  • proof-of-completion submission;
  • payment processing;
  • escrow-style payment holding;
  • dispute management;
  • ratings and reviews;
  • currency conversion for eligible international users; and
  • AI-powered assistance and recommendations.

Taska is not ordinarily the employer of a Tasker and does not itself perform the tasks posted

through the Platform.

2. DEFINITIONS

For these Terms:

  • “Task” means a unit of work posted on Taska with a description, budget and, where applicable, deadline.
  • “Task Poster” or “Poster” means a person or business that posts or commissions a Task.
  • “Tasker” means a person who offers to perform Tasks through Taska.
  • “Bid” means a Tasker’s offer to perform a Task for a stated amount and within a stated delivery

period.

  • “Task Agreement” means the agreement formed between a Poster and Tasker when a Bid or

fixed-price offer is accepted.

  • “Proof of Completion” means evidence submitted by a Tasker showing that a Task has been

completed, which may include photographs, video, written reports or geotagged information.

  • “Escrow” means the payment-holding arrangement under which funds paid for a Task are held

pending completion, approval, dispute resolution or another permitted release/refund event.

  • “Diaspora Mode” means Taska’s functionality allowing eligible users outside Nigeria to post,

track and pay for Tasks being performed in Nigeria.

  • “Platform Fee” means the commission charged by Taska in connection with completed Tasks.
  • “FX” means foreign exchange conversion between currencies.
  • “KYC” means Know Your Customer identity verification.

7. TASK POSTING AND BIDDING

8. FORMATION OF A TASK AGREEMENT

A Task Agreement is formed when a Poster accepts a Tasker’s Bid or otherwise accepts a Tasker’s

offer through the Platform.

Once accepted:

  • the Tasker is expected to perform the Task in accordance with the agreed requirements;
  • the Poster is expected to provide reasonable cooperation necessary for completion;
  • the agreed price becomes the basis for payment, subject to applicable fees and adjustments

expressly permitted under these Terms; and

  • the parties must communicate through Taska where reasonably necessary to document Task-

related communications.

Taska is not a party to the underlying Task Agreement between Poster and Tasker.

11. COMPLETION AND PAYMENT RELEASE

Once the Tasker submits the required Proof of Completion, the Poster may review the submitted

evidence.

11.1 Early Release

If the Poster confirms satisfactory completion, the applicable funds may be released to the Tasker

after deduction of applicable Taska fees and other permitted charges.

11.2 Automatic Release After 48 Hours

If the Tasker has submitted the required Proof of Completion, the Task has been marked as

completed, and the Poster does not approve the Task or open a dispute, the applicable funds will be

eligible for automatic release 48 hours after the applicable completion/proof submission event,

subject to any payment, fraud, compliance or technical review reasonably required by Taska.

11.3 Dispute Exception

Funds will not be automatically released where a valid dispute has been opened before the

applicable release time.

Where a dispute is opened, the funds will remain frozen until the dispute is resolved in accordance

with these Terms.
