Questions

Before you get started

How does escrow actually work? When you accept a Tasker for your task, your payment moves into a secure holding account. It only reaches the Tasker after you review their proof of completion and approve it.
What if the work isn't done properly? You can decline to approve and raise a dispute. Our resolution team reviews the proof of completion, messages, and both accounts before any funds move.
Do I need to verify my identity to post a task? Posters need a verified phone number. Taskers additionally verify a government ID before accepting paid work — this protects everyone posting tasks.
Can I use Taska from outside Nigeria? Yes. Many Posters manage tasks remotely for family or property back home. Payment and Tasker verification stay the same either way.
