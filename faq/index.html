Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about how Taska works.

For Posters

What is Taska?

Taska connects you with verified people in Nigeria who can carry out everyday tasks: repairs, errands, cleaning, generator servicing, deliveries and more. You post what you need, choose someone verified, and your payment is held securely until the work is done and you have approved it.

How much does it cost to post a task?

Posting is free. You set the price for the task, or agree it with the Tasker. Taska takes 10% from the Tasker's payment. Posters pay nothing beyond the agreed task price, There are no subscriptions yet and no listing fees.

Where does my money go when I pay?

Into escrow, held by Paystack, not into the Tasker's account and not into ours. It is released only after you review the proof of completion and approve. If you don't approve, it doesn't move.

How do I know the Tasker is who they say they are?

Every Tasker submits a government-issued ID (NIN, Voter's Card or Driver's Licence) and the name must match their account. We verify their phone number and address. In our first phase, every single Tasker is also verified by a member of our team on a video call. Their completed work and ratings stay permanently attached to their profile.

Can I post a task from outside Nigeria?

Yes, and this is what Taska was built for. You post from wherever you are, choose someone verified near the person or property that needs help, and receive photo proof before you release payment. You never have to send money to someone and hope.

What counts as proof that the task was done?

Timestamped photos, and where relevant a short video, uploaded by the Tasker before they can request payment. For something like a document collection, a photo of the document. For a repair, before-and-after images. You see all of it before you approve anything.

How quickly will someone accept my task?

It depends on the category and area. We aim for tasks to be claimed within a few hours during the working day. If nobody has claimed yours after [12 hours], contact us and we will find someone directly.

What if nobody accepts my task at all?

You are not charged. Nothing leaves escrow, and you can cancel and receive your money back in full. You can also repost at a different price or a wider time window.

Which areas do you cover?

We currently currently cover all states in Nigeria, with plans to expand to other countries in the future...but people from other countries can still sign up as Posters and Post tasks they want people to carry out for them in Nigeria.

What kinds of tasks can I post?

Home repairs and maintenance, generator and inverter servicing, cleaning, errands and deliveries, shopping runs, document pickups, property check-ins, and remote work such as data entry, design and virtual assistance. Anything illegal, unsafe or requiring a licence we don't verify is not permitted.

Can I request the same Tasker again?

Yes. If someone does good work, you can post directly to them for future tasks. Most people settle on two or three Taskers they trust and keep using them.

Can I cancel a task?

Yes. Before a Tasker claims it, cancel freely and you are refunded in full. After it is claimed but before work begins, you can still cancel, though we ask you to message the Tasker first. [State your policy for cancelling after work has started, including any partial payment.]

For Taskers

How do I join as a Tasker?

Sign up at taska.com.ng/signup, choose your categories and area, and submit your NIN or other government ID for verification. We will verify you personally. Once approved, tasks in your area appear and you can claim the ones you want.

Do I have to pay to join?

No. Registration is free and there are no subscription fees. [Our first 50 Taskers pay zero commission for their first 90 days.]

How and when do I get paid?

The client's money is already in escrow before you start, so the funds exist before you lift a spanner. When you finish, upload your proof, and once the client approves, payment is released to your bank account [within X hours]. No chasing anybody for balance.

How much commission does Taska take?

10% of the task value, deducted at payout.** Nothing else. You always see your net earnings before you claim a task.

What if a client refuses to approve work I completed properly?

Raise a dispute. Your photo proof and your messages are the evidence, which is why uploading clear before-and-after photos matters. A person on our team reviews it. Clients cannot simply keep the money and take the work; the funds remain in escrow until the dispute is resolved.
